# Sample credits

The non-synthesised noise sources of Peilung: a brook, a cow and rain, all recordings from
Freesound. The license is the recording's, checked on Freesound itself (2026-10-29; rain
2026-11-24).

**Changes:** cut to 1.5 s, downmixed to mono, faded, converted to MP3 (96 kbit/s) with ffmpeg.
Brook from 30.0 s (fades 50 ms in, 150 ms out), cow from 5.35 s (fades 20 ms in, 300 ms out),
rain from 8.0 s of the HQ preview (fades 50 ms in, 150 ms out).
