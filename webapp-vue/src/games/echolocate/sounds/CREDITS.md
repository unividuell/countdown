# Sample credits

The two non-synthesised noise sources of Peilung: a brook and a cow, both recordings from
Freesound. The license is the recording's, checked on Freesound itself (2026-10-29).

**Changes:** cut to 1.5 s, downmixed to mono, faded, converted to MP3 (96 kbit/s) with ffmpeg.
Brook from 30.0 s (fades 50 ms in, 150 ms out), cow from 5.35 s (fades 20 ms in, 300 ms out).
